The product

A control plane
for credible experiments.

Freeze

Bind the question, method, data, seeds, gates, budget, and stop rules.

Admit

Require forecasts, funding, role separation, security, and executor fit.

Settle

Resolve the exact contract and append evidence to a hash-linked history.
