## Frozen question

Can the unchanged Solomon sparse integer class head route five operations from
held-out sentence-template families after the first-token shortcut is removed
and each input receives a balanced wrong-operation distractor?

A `go` required every seed to reach 900,000 overall exact-rate ppm, every
seed-class pair to reach 800,000 ppm, and cross-seed agreement to reach 950,000
ppm. A valid miss on any threshold was `no_go`, not execution failure.

## What changed after EXP-007

EXP-007 used operation-specific command prefixes and passed perfectly. EXP-008
kept the same model geometry and training rule but changed the information
surface:

- all 11,000 inputs begin with `Route this quantity case.`;
- the five classes are exactly balanced, making the prefix-only rate 200,000
  ppm;
- each actual case is paired with a balanced distractor from a wrong operation;
- literal operation identifiers are absent from inputs; and
- 30 training template families and 20 promotion template families are
  normalized-surface disjoint.

The model remained an 8,192-feature signed integer perceptron using hashed
first-token, token, positioned-token, and byte 1–4-gram counts. Seeds 1–3 used
zero initialization, four epochs, and fixed shuffled training orders. All
three reached 1,000,000 ppm on training-only liveness checks before promotion
evaluation opened.

## Forecast settlement

Two 10-credit forecasts were frozen before the run:

| Forecaster | Go | No-go | Failure | Outcome | Multiclass Brier |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Mechanistic transfer | 72% | 27% | 1% | `no_go` | 1.0514 |
| Empirical training liveness | 82% | 17% | 1% | `no_go` | 1.3614 |

Both forecasts were overconfident in training exactness as evidence for
held-out transfer. This is useful calibration: exact fit and deterministic
replay did not imply template-invariant routing.

## Conclusion and next boundary

The unchanged sparse class-head path is closed for this shortcut-resistant
capability claim. The no-go does not reject all sparse models, all integer
models, arithmetic generation, or the wider Solomon lineage. It shows that
this fixed hashed feature map, perceptron objective, and four-epoch training
rule did not produce stable transfer across the held-out templates.

The result also validates the exploration method: EXP-007's perfect but
surface-specific result led to a deliberately discriminating successor, and
the successor exposed the generalization failure in one run. Do not tune this
model against the opened promotion set. A future branch must use a new frozen
evaluation surface and change the representation or learning objective—for
example, explicitly separate actual-case evidence from distractor context—and
should include a read-only representation-sufficiency diagnostic before new
training.
