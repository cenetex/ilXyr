## Frozen question

Does the Q2.6 global replay-tangent intervention reproduce on seeds 1 and 3
under the unchanged gates?

EXP-004 earned this replication step prospectively. Diagnostic seed 2 committed
700/700 full-scale attempts, produced six jointly feasible public checkpoints,
selected update 500 at 1.1833% replay regression, and passed the disjoint
promotion split. That was strong seed-level evidence, not yet a family result.

## What changed

Only the declared training seed changed. Seed 1 and seed 3 had separate
forecasts, funding, source executions, results, and provenance records.

## What remained frozen

- ZERO.3 initialization and all teacher, quantity, replay, public, and promotion
  artifacts.
- The global all-six-slice replay-tangent projection and descending scales from
  1 through 1/128.
- The direct 1.5% cumulative commit authority and 2% public replay ceiling.
- All quantity gates, optimizer settings, attempt budgets, checkpoint cadence,
  frontier rule, and stop conditions.
- Promotion opened exactly once only after a jointly feasible public checkpoint.
- A completed non-pass was valid `no_go`; execution and envelope failures remained
  separate.

Both seeds had to execute even if the first was no-go. Early family stopping and
adaptive repair were forbidden.

## Frozen family rule

The Q2.6 family resolved `go` if and only if seeds 1, 2, and 3 each resolved
`go`. Aggregation waited for both new results. If the conjunction passed, the
already-selected seed-2 quantized model with SHA-256
`44b32f2262be2754fd2eeaf16ed206bae32b4ce30d7f5541a1059cd21257ae50`
was the only ZERO.4 promotion candidate. Replication checkpoints could not
replace it through post-hoc selection.

## Decision

EXP-005 resolved `go` upstream. Seeds 1 and 3 both completed their one accepted
execution and passed the unchanged public and promotion gates. Together with
the prospectively settled seed-2 result from EXP-004, all three declared seeds
are `go`. The frozen family conjunction therefore passed, and the seed-2 model
became promotion-eligible as ZERO.4.

## ilXyr family workflow

The single [`family.json`](../../examples/experiments/zero-q26r/family.json)
manifest shows the streamlined multi-seed workflow. It retains separate seed
contracts, forecasts, funding, runs, evidence, and settlements, then applies one
hash-bound family rule.

```bash
ilxyr family freeze /workspace/ledger path/to/family.json
ilxyr family check /workspace/ledger path/to/family.json
ilxyr family run /workspace/ledger path/to/family.json --execute
ilxyr family settle /workspace/ledger path/to/family.json
```

EXP-005 is terminal. Do not run its seed executors again. These commands are a
reference for future families and for importing already-published evidence into
a new local ledger. The local repository has verified the upstream evidence,
but has not yet imported and settled EXP-005 in its own ilXyr ledger.
