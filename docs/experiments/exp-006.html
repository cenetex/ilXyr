Decision

The implementation is executable. This run shows no HRR advantage.

Claim boundary

What this result does—and does not—establish.

  • It verifies that the public Holo HRR implementation executes its declared T=512 comparison end to end on the public enwik8 corpus.
  • It does not reproduce the unpublished Telegram corpus or the upstream MPS hardware conditions.
  • Its promoted protocol lane applies to the recovered evidence transaction, not to a claim that HRR outperformed softmax.
  • It covers one seed on one CPU host; exact replication is retired, and broader performance claims are not supported.
